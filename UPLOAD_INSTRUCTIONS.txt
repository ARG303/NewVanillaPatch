NewVanilla / Launcher 0.6.0

Upload/replace in ARG303/NewVanillaPatch:
  LAUNCHER_VERSION
  MOD_VERSION
  manifest.json
  launcher/RA3FPSLauncher.exe
  mod/NewVanilla.zip
  .github/workflows/update-manifest.yml

After upload, the old launcher 0.5.1 can update itself to 0.6.0.
After 0.6.0 restarts it will download/install NewVanilla 1.00 and launch RA3 using:
  NewVanilla/NewVanilla_1.00.skudef

For future mod updates:
  1. Replace mod/NewVanilla.zip
  2. Change MOD_VERSION (example 1.00 -> 1.01)
  3. Commit
GitHub Actions recalculates SHA-256 and reads the .skudef filename from the ZIP automatically.
